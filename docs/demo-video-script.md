# Demo script (5 minutes live, or 2-3 minutes as video)

`docs/demo.mp4` follows this with on-screen captions (re-record: `python -m scripts.record_demo` against a fresh start).

| # | Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20s | Login | "How many competitor websites do you check each week, and how long does it take?" |
| 2 | 40s | Overview | The four numbers; "where you stand" bars; today's biggest changes. |
| 3 | 40s | Products | Point at red cells ("they're cheaper"), then *Only where I'm more expensive* sorted by biggest gap. |
| 4 | 50s | Product detail (Voltix 20W charger) | History chart; "ChargeZone dropped this by 7% this morning"; timeline; the "matching would cost" card. |
| 5 | 30s | Alerts | The feed and the rules; "this also goes to your WhatsApp". |
| 6 | 20s | Match review | Press **Yes**: "new competitor listings are matched in seconds". |
| 7 | 20s | Reports | Morning email on the phone preview; weekly summary PDF. |
| 8 | 30s | Close | "Give me 20 of your products and I'll track them free for two weeks." |

## Before a live demo
- [ ] Fresh start so "today" has a live check (Overview says "Prices checked … 100% read")
- [ ] 4 matches waiting in Match review
- [ ] Phone ready to show the morning email
- [ ] If possible, a few of the prospect's own products tracked against their real competitors (start 1-2 weeks before)
- [ ] Latest numbers from `docs/results.md`
