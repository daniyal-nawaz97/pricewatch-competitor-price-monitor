# Pitch and outreach: PriceWatch

**Who to message:** online stores (Daraz sellers, Shopify stores, Instagram shops with a website), retail chains and distributors, brands worried about unauthorised discounting.

**Strongest hook:** a live sample on the prospect's own products. Pick 10-20 of their products, find them on 2-3 competitor sites, add them, and send a screenshot of their products table after a few days.

## First message

**A. English**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], I set up a system that checks your competitors' prices every day and sends you a short report when anyone changes theirs. Can I track 20 of your products for two weeks, free, so you can see it?

**B. With a finding (best)**
> Hi [Name], I tracked 15 of your best sellers against [Competitor A] and [Competitor B] this week. On 6 of them you're the most expensive, by up to 12%. I can send you this every morning automatically. Want the free 2-week version?

**C. Roman Urdu**
> Assalam o Alaikum [Name], maine aik system banaya hai jo roz aap ke competitors ki prices check karta hai aur jab koi price badle to subah aik chhoti report bhej deta hai. Aap ke 20 products do hafte free track kar ke dikhaun?

Attach: the morning email screenshot (`screenshots/email.png`) or the 75-second demo video.

## Follow-ups
- **3-4 days:** "Here's what the morning report looks like on a phone: [image]. Still happy to set up your 20 products free."
- **A week:** "Last note: if competitor pricing ever costs you sales, reply 'track' with 20 product links and I'll do the rest."

## Objections

| They say | You answer |
|---|---|
| "I can check manually." | For 100+ products and 3 competitors that's 300 pages, about 4 hours each time. This does it every morning before you wake up. |
| "What if a site changes?" | The data-health page shows each site's success rate daily; fixing broken sites is part of the monthly plan. |
| "Is this allowed?" | Only public pages, once a day, slowly, identifying itself and respecting robots.txt. For important contracts, check with a lawyer. |
| "Their products have different names." | Matching handles naming differences; anything uncertain goes to a quick Yes/No review. |
| "Will it change my prices?" | No. It informs your decisions; you decide. (Repricing suggestions are an add-on.) |
