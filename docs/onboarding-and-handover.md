# Client onboarding, handover and maintenance

## Onboarding (after they say yes)
1. **Product list**: get name, SKU, price, category (and cost if they want margin) as CSV → Settings → Products & import.
2. **Competitors**: agree the list and add each one, with its catalogue page if it has one.
3. **Matching**: add product pages (Track a page) or confirm suggestions in Match review. Check variants (colour, size, storage) carefully.
4. **Test reading**: every new page is read once when added; fix any site that needs a price selector.
5. **Spot check**: compare 20 products by hand with the dashboard. Write the result down; it goes in the case study.
6. **Rules & reports**: alert thresholds, delivery channels, report time and recipients.
7. **Run a week**, review the results together, then go live with a short walkthrough video.

## Handover checklist
- [ ] Demo competitors removed; client's competitors and products loaded
- [ ] Spot check of 20 products done and recorded
- [ ] Email (SMTP) and any WhatsApp/Telegram delivery tested
- [ ] `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` set so "Open dashboard" links work
- [ ] Team members added (admin / analyst / viewer)
- [ ] Maintenance terms agreed in writing: how fast broken sites get fixed (e.g. within 2 working days)

## Weekly maintenance (10 minutes)
- Competitors page: any card amber or red? Open the error, fix the selector or URL.
- Match review: confirm new listings.
- Products with "no data": the competitor may have removed the page; remove or replace the listing.
