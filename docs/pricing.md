# Packages and pricing (suggested; adjust to your market)

| Package | Monthly | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | PKR 12,000 | Up to 50 products, 3 competitors, daily morning report, in-app alerts |
| **Business** | PKR 30,000 | Up to 500 products, 10 competitors, alerts by email/WhatsApp/Telegram, weekly summary, team access |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Thousands of products, marketplaces, API & Google Sheets sync, pricing suggestions, priority fixes |

- **Setup fee:** PKR 15,000-40,000 (product import, competitor pages, matching, selectors for difficult sites).
- **Pilot:** 2 weeks tracking 20 products, free or low cost, then convert to monthly.
- **Add-ons:** WhatsApp alerts, weekly analyst summary call, extra competitors.
- **Your costs:** a small always-on server. No API fees.

Landing-page prices live in `static/landing.html`.
